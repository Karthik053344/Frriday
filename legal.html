Shipping Policy

FRRIDAY products are made to order through a fulfillment partner. Available shipping methods, costs and estimated delivery dates are shown during checkout based on the destination and products in the order.

Processing

Orders may require production time before shipment because products are made to order. The applicable estimate is presented during checkout where available.

Shipping

Shipping rates depend on destination, product type, quantity and the available fulfillment route. An order containing different products may be fulfilled in separate shipments.

Tracking

Where tracking is available, tracking information will be provided through the order or shipping notification.

Delays

Delivery estimates are estimates rather than guaranteed delivery dates. Carrier delays, production issues, weather, customs processing and other circumstances outside FRRIDAY's control may affect delivery.

International orders

International customers may be responsible for customs duties, import taxes or other charges imposed by the destination country where applicable.

Incorrect addresses

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address. Contact FRRIDAY as soon as possible if you notice an address error.

Returns & Refunds

FRRIDAY products may be produced specifically for an order. Because of this, returns for change of mind or incorrect size may be limited. The applicable return conditions should be reviewed before purchase.

Wrong or damaged item

If your order arrives damaged, defective or materially different from what you ordered, contact FRRIDAY as soon as reasonably possible with your order information and clear photographs of the issue.

Incorrect item

If you receive the wrong product, size or color, contact FRRIDAY before returning anything so the appropriate resolution can be determined.

Change of mind

Because products may be made to order, change-of-mind returns may not be available for every product. Any applicable exceptions will be communicated through the store.

Refunds

Where a refund is approved, it will normally be processed through the original payment method. Processing time can depend on the payment provider.

Please contact FRRIDAY before sending a product back. Unapproved returns may not be accepted.
